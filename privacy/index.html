ilyCal Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ilyCal handles personal information in its iPhone and Android apps and in its account, backup, and shared calendar services. You can use a personal calendar on your device without creating an account.

Information we collect and use

ilyCal stores plans, profiles, preferences, drafts, and reminder settings on your device to provide your calendar. Signing in by itself does not upload your personal calendar. Shared calendar sync and optional iPhone account backups send the information described below to ilyCal’s services.

If you create an account, we use your email address and account identifier to sign you in, confirm your email address, help you recover access, and secure your account. When you choose Google sign-in or Sign in with Apple, the provider may also return basic profile information. Apple may supply a relay email address. Our service providers may process network and security information, such as your IP address and request time.

Connected calendars and permissions

With your device’s calendar permission, ilyCal reads events from calendars you connect. When you save an event to a connected calendar, its provider handles the event under that calendar’s sync and sharing settings. You can disconnect a calendar in ilyCal or revoke access in device settings. The original events remain with the provider. Hiding a calendar changes what appears in ilyCal. Its events may still count toward availability or be shared under your existing settings.

Google sign-in gives ilyCal no access to Gmail or Google Calendar. On iPhone, Apple’s calendar sheet handles invitation replies through the calendar account configured on your device. ilyCal reads the saved response from the device calendar. The calendar provider handles syncing the response and notifying the organizer under its own settings.

Shared calendars and who can see your data

Supabase provides account authentication, storage, and shared calendar sync. People with access to a shared calendar can see its shared plans, profiles, and published availability. Members with editing access can make changes. Manage members, invitations, and calendar sharing in Together.

You choose what ilyCal shares from a connected calendar. Only me keeps its events private within ilyCal. Busy shares time blocks without titles, locations, or notes. Event details can include titles and locations, and includes notes if you enable them. Changes made offline take effect for other members after they sync.

Photos, widgets, and notifications

On iPhone, you can choose a profile photo or use your camera or photo library to share a Moment. On Android, you can choose a photo from your library to share a Moment. ilyCal prepares the selected image and removes image metadata before uploading a Moment. Members can view shared Moments in the app and in iPhone Home Screen widgets. A Moment’s 24-hour display window begins when its upload completes. Expired images are removed from new views and queued for cleanup. Device caches and widget refreshes can take longer. The author or a calendar organizer can remove a Moment earlier. Failed uploads stay on your device until retried successfully or discarded. Other people can save copies or take screenshots.

iPhone widgets use a device-local cache of upcoming plans and active photo thumbnails. The Android agenda widget uses a device-local cache of upcoming visible plans. Reminders and notification previews can show event information on your device. On Android, reminder choices stay on your device, and precise reminders use optional Alarms & reminders access. Connected calendar alerts on Android remain managed by your calendar app. You can change notification permissions in device settings.

Place searches

When you request location suggestions on iPhone, your search text and selected city may be sent to Apple Maps. ilyCal does not request access to your device’s location. A location you save with a plan can be included in shared event details or an optional iPhone account backup.

Security, backups, and exports

ilyCal relies on your device’s protections for locally stored calendar data and secures sign-in credentials using iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. On Android, pending invitation links are stored in encrypted app storage until used, discarded, or expired. Shared calendar data is encrypted in transit and at rest. ilyCal’s servers can read shared calendar data to provide sync.

If you enable account backup on iPhone, ilyCal saves plans, profiles, profile photos, shared blocks, history, and preferences to your account. Connected-calendar names and settings help you reconnect. The backup excludes private imported event details and the Moments photo cache. New backups are encrypted on your phone before upload. You need your recovery key to restore one on another device. The service can see backup metadata such as save times and file size. Older readable backups can be upgraded in Backup & restore.

Both apps let you export calendar data to a file you choose, which may be stored by a cloud provider. Exported files can be read by anyone who has access to them. Android excludes ilyCal’s app data from system cloud backup and device transfer.

Retention and deletion

Turning off automatic iPhone account backup keeps the saved snapshots. Use Delete saved backup in Backup & restore to remove the current and previous cloud snapshots. A device that still has automatic backup enabled can create a new one.

On iPhone, you can request account deletion in Settings → Your account → Manage account. On Android, open Settings → Your account → Delete account. If your account is linked to Apple, you can confirm deletion in ilyCal on an iPhone. You can also request account deletion on the web. Account deletion removes your linked sign-in records and private account backups. Personal calendars saved on your device and records that belong to a shared calendar can remain. Revoking access prevents further authorized sync. Copies that others already downloaded, exported, or saved can remain on their devices.

We retain a limited confirmation record so an offline device can verify that an account deletion completed. It contains account and request identifiers, a hash used to verify the request, timestamps, and completion status. The record has no automatic expiry.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Advertising and tracking

ilyCal does not include advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not sell calendar or photo content.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can review or correct profile and calendar information in the app, export calendar data, change your sharing choices, or withdraw device permissions. You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Keep passwords, verification codes, and private calendar content out of your email.

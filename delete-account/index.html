Delete your ilyCal account

You can request deletion of your ilyCal account and associated account data even if you no longer have the app.

Request deletion by email

Email an account deletion request from the address associated with your ilyCal account. If you used a private relay address, include it if you know it. Keep your password and recovery key private.

Delete in the app

On iPhone, go to Settings → Your account → Manage account → Delete account. On Android, go to Settings → Your account → Delete account. An account linked to Apple can complete the Apple confirmation on an iPhone or use the email request above.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes your linked sign-in records and private account backups. Personal calendars stored on your devices and plans that belong to a shared calendar can remain. See the privacy policy for retention details.

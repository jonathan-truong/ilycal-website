# Landing page image sources

## App screenshots

`agenda-week.png`, `week-board.png`, `month.png`, `day-sheet.png`, `detail.png` and `smart-entry.webp` are
unedited iPhone 18 Pro simulator captures of a Debug build of ilyCal (November 14, 2026), downscaled to
644 × 1400. The calendar shown is a fictional household, “The Lindens” (Nora, Sam, Juno, Theo), loaded
through Settings → Calendar → Import calendar data and kept on the device with no account signed in.

## Profile photos

## Brand and type

`app-icon.png` and `icon-180.png` are the app icon from `native/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`. Figtree and Hedvig Letters Serif are the fonts
bundled with the app (`native/Resources/Fonts/`), both under the SIL Open Font License; the licence texts
sit beside the font files.

`view-agenda.png` and `view-day.png` are further captures from the same build and calendar, used by the
Agenda / Day / Week / Month switcher.
